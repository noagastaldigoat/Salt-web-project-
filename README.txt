ONO FLAVORS HAWAIʻI — MONOCHROME WEBSITE CONCEPT

Open index.html in a browser to preview the responsive static prototype.

This pass applies the approved black-and-white visual direction across the shared stylesheet and homepage. Island identity comes through natural coastal and food photography, editorial spacing, and existing brand/product copy—not added accent colors. The homepage uses four products from the supplied catalog and does not show invented prices. Product photo areas remain neutral placeholders until approved real product images are supplied.

BEFORE LAUNCH
- Replace the text wordmark with the exact original client logo file; it has not been embedded in this package because a usable logo file was not available in the extracted site assets.
- Replace neutral product-photo placeholders with client-approved product images. Existing externally hosted editorial photos should be reviewed for licensing/usage rights and preferably replaced with approved brand photography.
- Confirm all contact information, social URLs, and policy details with the client.
- Prices are marked “Price to be confirmed”; do not enable purchase/checkout until current prices and product details are verified.
- Cart, checkout, contact form, and newsletter are prototype/demo interactions and require real service integrations before launch.
- Review page-specific SEO metadata, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, accessibility, and production performance before publishing.
